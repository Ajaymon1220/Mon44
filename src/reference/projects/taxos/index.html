The project at a glance

The problem
Document collection and manual workpapers slowed offshore teams preparing US tax returns.
My contribution
Designed the CPA workspace and client portal. Built a functional prototype with mock tax data to test workflows before production.
Product ambition
Reduce manual preparation work by at least 50%. This was the project goal, not an established result.
Scope and evidence notes

This story and its screenshots come from my earlier TaxOS case study. Research, domain expertise, engineering and commercial results were team efforts. Reported outcomes are retained below with their missing measurement context.

04 / BEYOND STATIC SCREENS

Make the workflow tangible.

I translated Figma designs into a functional prototype, using Claude Code for backend logic, GitHub for version control and Vercel for deployment. Mock tax data let CPAs try the flow before production development.

Design

Shape the workspace and client intake in Figma.

Prototype

Connect screens with working logic and mock tax data.

Learn

Test with CPAs and carry feedback into the next version.

Prototype work demonstrates interaction and workflow exploration; it does not establish the accuracy of AI-generated tax information.

05 / ITERATION

From a prototype to a second version.

Discovery & testing

Market research, domain mapping and high-fidelity exploration established the initial direction.

V1 & beta

Development and testing involved the CPA network recruited during discovery.

V2 & launch

The next version refined the dynamic questionnaire and bulk document matching before commercial launch.

The next detail to document

The earlier case study attributes these refinements to prototype feedback, but does not show a specific observation, before-and-after revision or retest result. Add that chain of evidence to show precisely how a CPA's feedback changed the design.

A proposed next evaluation: observe how CPAs detect and correct a mismatched document or uncertain extraction. Measure correction effort alongside preparation speed.

TaxOS launched as a revenue-generating SaaS product, according to the original case study. The design connects three previously scattered parts of preparation.
