Case study

Karbon Business

Karbon Business

A clearer, responsive web experience for a complex financial product

Client

Karbon Business

Role

UX and interface design

Year

2024 – Present

Discipline

Web design · Responsive UX · Visual design

Overview

A redesign of the Karbon Business web experience, focused on communicating the product clearly and helping visitors find the information they need.

The challenge

The existing experience needed stronger navigation, a more deliberate content hierarchy and a responsive interface that communicated the product's capabilities.

The approach

Research and discovery informed streamlined navigation, clearer typography and a mobile-first structure. Consistent visual patterns brought product information into a more cohesive story.

Design priorities

I focused on user-centric navigation, readable product information, responsive layouts and clear visual hierarchy, working across content and interface decisions.

Outcome

A more coherent web experience that makes a complex financial product easier to explore and understand.
