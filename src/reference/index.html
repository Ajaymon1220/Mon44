A note from Ajaymon

About me

Hi there,

I'm Ajaymon, a product designer based in Bangalore. I design intuitive, accessible experiences across web and mobile, with a focus on making complex workflows feel simple. At Karbon Business, I work on fintech products, from customer dashboards to internal bank operations tools.

My process connects user research, wireframes, prototypes and high-fidelity interfaces with design systems and developer handoff. I collaborate with product, engineering and business teams, test ideas with users, and follow through with design QA. My background in data engineering brings an analytical lens to design, while AI opens new ways to explore, prototype and solve problems.

Talk soon,

Ajaymon

How I think
